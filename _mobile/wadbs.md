**WADBS** (short or **Wireless Android Debugging Bridge Switch**) is an app that allows you to switch Android's Wireless Debugging feature on and off quickly.

It provides several widgets and a quick settings tile to do this.

You can also instantly connect to your PC using KDE Connect's clipboard sharing feature (see [scripts](./scripts)).

## Features

- **Quick settings tile.** Control wireless debugging state from the quick settings.
- **Widgets.** Widgets to control and monitor wireless debugging state from your home screen.
- **Widget customization.** Tools shouldn't be boring-looking, right?
- **Instant connection.** When configured, WADBS will automatically pair your Android device with your development PC.
- **Shizuku support.** Some features are available without root through Shizuku.
- **OneUI integration.** Even more QS fun on OneUI 2.1 (and older) thanks to the [One UI Project](https://oneuiproject.github.io/hidden-api/qs-detail-view/).
