Hello, readers from around the world! This post is an example to show off the articles section on the [Mobile Apps](/mobile.html) page of this website.

Here are some interesting facts about this website and the Android operating system:

- The Android jelly bean logo you see on the Mobile Apps page was taken from the archived version of the android.com website
- Android versions were usually named after sweet desserts, but starting from Android 10 Q Google decided to abandon this transition. Do you know a dessert name that starts from Q? This would be a wise reason to add comments on this website...
- Android is actually open source, but most device manufacturers ship a proprietary modification of the AOSP. Such modifications are sometimes referred to as "UI skins", but they are usually more than just visual overhauls. Every such modification not only brings its user a fancy UI, but also includes a burden of slightly changed system APIs.
